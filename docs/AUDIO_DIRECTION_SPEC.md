# Audio Direction Spec — original / royalty-free only

Per-chapter sound worlds. Constraint: everything original (procedural like the Vegas
house track) or verified royalty-free. No licensed samples, no copyrighted melodies.

## The rule under all of it
The music tracks JP's interior, not the room's genre. Every era has a surface sound
(what the room hears) and an undertow (what JP feels). The undertow is the mix priority.
JP's canon: the anxiety lives under the drive. The audio's job is to make that audible.

## Mixing / ducking rules
- Dialogue always ducks music -6dB minimum; JP's Mind lines duck harder (interior = closer).
- Silence is a mixed asset: Wrong Crowd pre-raid, Release, and the receipt screen get TRUE silence (no room tone) — reserve full mute for exactly these three so it stays devastating.
- Loudness target consistent across chapters (no volume jumps scene-to-scene); master pass at the end, not per-scene.
- Every required-action SFX (choice clicks, records) shares one family of sounds so "this mattered" is audible game-wide.

## Motif discipline
One JP motif (4-6 notes). It appears: unresolved in Home, thinned in SB, detuned in Weed Rise, absent in Wrong Crowd/Court, slowly re-tuning through Jail, held as the Release chord, worked against tempo at Caymus, resolving on first payment in Come Up, glossed in LA, buried under house in Vegas, and finally played complete and warm at Home Return. The whole life in one melody's repair.
