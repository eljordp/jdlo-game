## What these scores mean

These are current implementation scores, not predictions of potential and not
claims about universal quality. Each total is calculated from six anchored
levels. No chapter receives a `4 — Proven` because no blind-player evidence
exists yet.

Weights:

- Causal clarity (`CC`): 15
- Emotional truth (`ET`): 20
- Understanding JP (`UJ`): 20
- Player embodiment (`PE`): 15
- Documentary integrity (`DI`): 15
- Craft delivery (`CD`): 15

Formula: `sum(weight × level / 4)`, rounded to a whole number.

`Based on a true story.` is **UNSCORED — title card**. Home is the first scene
with enough content to evaluate.

## Does the current game communicate JP correctly?

**Mostly, but not safely enough to call proven.** The intended reading is:

> JP was young, hungry and misdirected. He made real choices and paid for them.
> Context explains those choices without erasing responsibility. The same
> intensity produced something different once it found discipline, faith and
> direction.

The current build supports that reading through the LUNA-to-farm chain, Pops
call, legal consequence, jail phases, Caymus labor and self-taught client work.

Four risks still distort it:

1. **The front half can glamorize competence.** If the weed loop feels too fun
   and paranoia arrives too late, a player may admire the operation more than
   understand the narrowing choices around it.
2. **Jail can become a redemption montage.** If survival and failure do not
   drag at normal speed, books, fitness and faith will feel like an upgrade
   sequence instead of an earned turn inside hell.
3. **The back half can become self-mythology.** LA, Operator and Vegas sometimes
   show access, work and lifestyle before they show cost, doubt or unfinished
   responsibility.
4. **Women can read as atmosphere or reward.** Nikki has causal agency, but
   party/club women need believable motives and boundaries so they communicate
   JP's world rather than functioning as proof that he made it.

The goal is not for every player to decide JP is a good person. The successful
result is that they cannot reduce him to either villain or hero, can explain
why the choices became reasonable in the moment, and still recognize which
choices were his.

That mean is an orientation tool, not the main result. The useful ordering is:

- strongest current chapters: Santa Barbara, Weed Rise, Wrong Crowd, Come Up;
- middle: Court, Locked Up, Release, Caymus, Ending;
- clearest weaknesses: LA, Vegas player ownership, Home/Operator craft delivery.

## Evidence behind each score

### Home — 66

- `CC 3`: North Bay family pressure, school expectations and early appetite are understandable.
- `ET 2`: warmth exists, but dense dialogue sometimes tells the conflict twice.
- `UJ 3`: family, Ivy, Pops and personal objects explain the person before the fall.
- `PE 3`: the player explores, checks objects and participates in the house routines.
- `DI 3`: corrected schools, LUNA scale and personal details match the ledger.
- `CD 2`: some large rooms still feel staged and the opening has not been timing-tested.

### Santa Barbara — 75

- All dimensions `3`: it has place identity, fun, social pull, loss, a normal dinner,
  a private opportunity and a player-owned decision. The chapter is not `4` because
  strangers have not confirmed that the choice feels understandable rather than forced.

### Weed Rise — 75

- All dimensions `3`: supply, weighing, calls, deliveries, money, comfort and paranoia
  form a playable loop. Its main unknown is full-run repetition and optional-content load.

### Wrong Crowd — 75

- All dimensions `3`: the Pops call, surveillance, route tension, raid silence and Jose
  beat make consequence embodied. Blind players must still prove whether dread is clear
  without prior knowledge.

### Court — 71

- `PE 2`: the lack of power is part of the scene, but the player mostly receives the plea.
- Every other dimension `3`: the 13-years-to-one-year framing is legible, restrained and
  preserves mystery while recording that JP never intended to hurt anyone.

### Locked Up — 71

- `CC/ET/UJ/PE/DI 3`: old habits, fighting, the denied appeal/Bird realization, yard
  respect, commissary protection, routine, books and faith support the change.
- `CD 2`: a normal-speed cell-to-release run is still missing, and the new chow hall is
  built and compiled but not yet player-verified.

### Release — 71

- `PE 2`: it is a short authored release, not a systems chapter.
- All other dimensions `3`: faith is carried out without claiming vindication.

### Caymus — 70

- `ET 2`: physical labor and frustration exist, but the AI transition remains the most
  delicate tonal handoff.
- All other dimensions `3`: vineyard identity, $20-ish work, coworkers, tractor and crash
  make the post-jail chapter understandable and playable.

### Come Up — 75

- All dimensions `3`: fixing sites, triaging clients, pricing, ghosting and collecting the
  first legitimate payment show self-teaching with real friction.

### LA — 59

- `CC/DI 3`: the chapter's place in the timeline and factual pressure are understandable.
- `ET/UJ/PE/CD 2`: it remains montage-heavy, gives the player limited ownership and does
  not yet reveal enough beyond “work continues inside the lifestyle.”

### Operator — 66

- `CC/UJ/PE/DI 3`: real projects, open work, scope and client pressure replace fake success.
- `ET/CD 2`: emotional friction and workplace silhouette still lag behind the stronger maps.

### Vegas — 63

- `CC/DI/CD 3`: authentic crew, venue sequence, house sound, lighting and motion are legible.
- `ET/UJ/PE 2`: it is still more impressive to watch than revealing to inhabit.

### Home Return — 68

- `CC/ET/UJ/DI 3`: Ivy, Mom, sister and required Pops reunion complete the family arc.
- `PE/CD 2`: traversal is light and some large interiors remain visually staged.

### Ending — 71

- `CC/ET/UJ/PE/DI 3`: the receipt compares the player's choices with JP's without inventing
  an alternate history.
- `CD 2`: the emotional ending still competes with replay/share/follow surfaces.

## What can change these numbers

The first uninterrupted run can move `CD`, `PE` and `ET`. Three blind players
can unlock a `4 — Proven` or expose an intended effect that never arrived.

The most important result is not “did the average rise?” It is whether players:

- understand why JP chose the weed opportunity without believing he had no agency;
- feel jail as cost rather than a transformation flex;
- recognize faith as gained rather than divine vindication;
- describe JP with more complexity after the receipt than before it.
